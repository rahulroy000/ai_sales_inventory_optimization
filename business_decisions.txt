1 Which products and categories generate the most revenue and profit?

    - Visualization: Ranked bar chart, Pareto chart.
    - Business decision: Identify top-performing products and categories and understand their contribution to business profitability.

2 How do sales and units sold change over time?

    - Visualization: Monthly revenue and units-sold line charts.
    - Business decision: Identify growth trends, seasonal patterns, and sales declines.

3 Which products have high demand but low stock?

    - Visualization: Demand-versus-stock scatter plot.
    - Business decision: Identify products that may need urgent replenishment.

4 Which products are below their reorder level?

    - Visualization: Stock-risk bar chart and product table.
- Business decision: Prioritize products at risk of stockouts.

5 Which platforms perform best?

    - Visualization: Revenue and units-sold comparison charts.
    - Business decision: Understand sales-channel performance.

6 How are discounts associated with sales and profit margins?

    - Visualization: Discount-versus-sales scatter plot, discount-band comparison, and margin analysis.
    - Business decision: Evaluate discount effectiveness and its relationship with sales volume and profitability.

7 Which suppliers and logistics partners have longer or more variable delivery times?

    - Visualization: Histogram, box plot, and ranked delivery-time bar chart.
    - Business decision: Identify supply-chain delays and partners requiring investigation.

8 Which categories or products have the most unstable demand?

    - Visualization: Demand variability chart and product demand time series.
    - Business decision: Identify products requiring closer forecasting and inventory control.

9 What quantity should we order for each product?

    - Analysis: Demand forecasting, reorder-point calculation, and safety-stock assessment.
    - Visualization: Forecast and reorder-point chart, recommended-order table.
    - Business decision: Recommend replenishment quantities while accounting for lead times, current stock, and minimum order quantities.

10 How accurate are our sales forecasts?

    - Visualization: Actual-versus-predicted line chart and forecast error comparison.
    - Metrics: MAE, RMSE, and WAPE where appropriate.
    - Business decision: Determine whether forecasts are reliable enough to support inventory decisions.

11 Which states have the highest product demand?

    - Visualization: Ranked bar chart.
    - Business decision: Identify high-demand geographic markets and potential distribution priorities.

12 Which weekday and product-category combinations generate the most sales?

    - Visualization: Weekday-versus-category heatmap.
    - Business decision: Identify weekly sales patterns to support staffing, promotions, and inventory planning.

13 Which numeric variables are related to one another?

    - Visualization: Correlation heatmap.
    - Business decision: Identify relationships worth investigating further and potential features for forecasting models.

14 Which individual products generate the most revenue and units sold?

    - Visualization: Ranked bar chart and Pareto chart.
    - Business decision: Identify high-contribution products and prioritize product-level analysis.

15 Which logistics partners have the highest order cancellation rates?

    - Visualization: Ranked bar chart.
    - Business decision: Identify logistics partners associated with frequent cancellations and investigate possible operational causes.

16 Does shipping distance influence delivery time?

    - Visualization: Scatter plot, optionally supplemented by a box plot of delivery time across distance bands.
    - Business decision: Understand the relationship between shipping distance and delivery performance.
